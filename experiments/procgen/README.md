# procgen -- the procedural asset kit

State: frozen on 2026-09-12, works.  Moved here from `tools/` when the
chunk pipeline (`tools/xm2dsm.lua`, `Makefile.assets`) became the way
assets reach the PS2.  Kept because the generators, the agent-artist
workflow and the town are worth coming back to: they should one day
write `.xm` models instead of `.xpl` prim lists, and the town should
become a real scene.

## What is here

* `xpl.py` -- the `.xpl` prim list file: writer, parser, PNG previewer.
  An `.xpl` is a 16 byte header and the raw DMA chain `xtcpBuildList`
  records, nolight pipeline; verified batch-exact against a PS2 recording.
* `xplkit.py` -- the authoring library the generators use: primitives,
  sweeps, polyhedra, noise, blobs, a baked lighting rig (`Build(DAYLIGHT)`).
* `townplan.py` -- the layout program that writes `town.scene`;
  `scenepreview.py` software-renders a `.scene` on the PC.
* `obj2inc.py`, `objcolor.py` -- Wavefront OBJ with the `v x y z r g b`
  colour extension into the C includes of `demos/xtcdemo` (`monkey.inc`),
  and a headless Blender script that makes such a file.
* `primdsm.py` -- the first prim-lists-as-dvp-as-text step, superseded
  by `xm2dsm.lua`; `src/data/monkey_std*.dsm` were generated by it and
  are checked in.
* `KITNOTES.md` -- what the artist agents reported: what worked, what to
  tell the next ones.
* `assets.txt`, `town.scene`, `sphere.xpl`, `clipbug.scene` -- the small
  inputs of xtcdemo's `plfile`, `town` and `file` scenes, which read this
  directory (`PROCGEN` in `demos/xtcdemo/scenes.c`).
* `assets/` (gitignored, 460 MB) -- the finished assets, each
  deterministic from its `gen_*.py` next to it, and `gallery.png`, an
  on-console contact sheet.  `ref/` (gitignored) -- reference images.

## The workflow that worked

Opus agents as artists, one creative lane each, driving the `xpl.py`
preview loop and judging the render with `shade=False` (the previewer's
headlight flatters dark bakes; the PS2 shows raw gouraud).  Constraints
that matter: +z up, alpha 255, colours are the whole look (bake the AO
and gradients), position-keyed noise so strip-join duplicates cannot
tear.  Town set assets are in metres, origin at the footprint centre,
ground at z=0, facing -y, small budgets because they are instanced.

## To pick it up

`python3 xpl.py --help` and `KITNOTES.md` first.  The PS2 side still
loads `.xpl` through `src/xfile.c`; when the examples load chunks
instead, that loader and the three scenes retire with it, and the
generators grow an `.xm` writer so `xm2dsm.lua` takes over from there.
