A gap in mdma, found while deciding whether to convert packVertices. streamWord computes off = bytePos(l)/4 and writes the VIF code there without first aligning the cursor to a word. After a narrow UNPACK body that isn't a whole number of words — V3_8 with a vertex count not a multiple of 4 is exactly this, which is why packVertices has while((uint32)i8p & 3) *i8p++ = 0; — the next VIF code silently lands at the wrong offset. mdmaBeginGifTag_ already checks for this and fails; streamWord doesn't. The fix is padding pendb up to a multiple of 4 at the top of streamWord, which is just your "padded to 32 bit words" rule enforced by the library. I didn't touch it — it's mdma's shape and you've been making those calls.
